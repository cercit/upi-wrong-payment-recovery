# Brag Plan: Wrong UPI Payment Recovery (PRD_Workshop)

## What is this app?
A PM case study and clickable prototype for PhonePe: when you send money to the wrong UPI ID, you get a one-tap recovery request, the recipient gets a one-tap return, and both can follow a live tracker. Concept only, not affiliated with PhonePe.

## The angle
The problem is a real panic moment that everyone in India has either lived or fears. The video stays on that moment and shows the fix in the order a user would meet it: the mistake, the maze, three taps, the answer. No jokes. The claim it earns is "here is a worked-out product answer, with a PRD, research and a prototype behind it".

## Hook (first 2-3 seconds)
A payment-success card for ₹4,500 slides in. The headline lands over it: "₹4,500 to the wrong UPI ID." (real numbers from the prototype's mock data.)

## Key moments (the middle)
- The maze: five places you have to chase one mistake (Recipient, Your bank, Their bank, NPCI, RBI Ombudsman), appearing one by one, then "Nobody owns your case."
- One-tap Recover: the receipt screen from the prototype, cursor taps "Recover Payment Now", it flips to "Recovery Claim Registered".
- Easy Return: recipient taps "Return ₹4,500", PIN dots fill, "returned".
- Live Tracker: four timeline steps arrive one by one, the third pulses "Within 3 hrs 15 mins", the fourth is the payoff.

## Outro / punchline
"Wrong UPI payment recovery." then "Get it back, step by step." with the live site address and the disclaimer.

## User flow worth showing
Entry: receipt with the Recover button. Key action: tap Recover, then the recipient's Return. Result: live tracker ending in "Automated Reversal & Credit".

- Creative direction: quiet product film for a PM case study, calm after the panic

- Role: warm bed with sparse professional accents
