Wrong UPI Payment Recovery Flow

A product concept and portfolio case study focused on reducing friction in accidental money transfer recovery, improving trust, and giving users a faster path to resolution.

Fintech UX Product Strategy PhonePe Recovery PM Portfolio

Project summary

Faster recovery One-tap action from the payment receipt.
Lower stress Clear status and next-step visibility.
Higher trust Transparent return flow and live progress updates.
Measurable impact Recovery success rate as the north star metric.
