# PRD to Sprint: plan

Source PRD: [PRD_Main.md](PRD_Main.md) (full PRD plus persona, empathy map and journey). The Google Doc copy is [PRD_Portfolio.md](PRD_Portfolio.md).
Method: the 4 Oct workshop guide ([session-guide.md](session-guide.md)), extended with team tasks, risk tracking and progress tracking.
Rule: each step waits for Sameer's OK before the next one starts.

| # | Step | What comes out | File |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | User stories | About 10 stories from PRD section 8, one action each, all passing INVEST | backlog.md |
| 2 | Acceptance criteria | A done-when checklist plus Gherkin for each story (at least 2 problem paths), and one Definition of Done for the whole team | backlog.md |
| 3 | Sizing | Effort, Risk and Impact scored 1–3 each, giving S/M/L/XL. Every XL gets split | backlog.md + backlog.csv (Sheets formulas) |
| 4 | Team tasks | Each story broken into tasks for Design, Android/iOS, Backend, QA, Data and Legal/Compliance | tasks.md |
| 5 | Sprint board | Priority order, a 12-point Sprint 1, and the 5 lists with WIP limits (In Progress 3, In Review 2), ready for Trello or Jira | board.md |
| 6 | Risks and AI audit | Senior-engineer review of the top 3 stories with findings triaged. A risk register: UDIR API, sponsor bank, legal sign-off, abuse | risks.md |
| 7 | Tracking | Daily check: points done against plan, blocked cards, risk status | tracking.md |
